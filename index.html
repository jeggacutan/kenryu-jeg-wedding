Honouring our heritage

Filipino Wedding Traditions

❧ ♡ ❧

We honour the beautiful traditions of a Filipino Catholic wedding, representing unity, faith, commitment and everlasting love.

🕯️ Unity Candle Ceremony

Two individual candles represent the lives of the bride and groom. Together, they light a single candle, symbolizing the beginning of their new life as one.

🤍 Veil Ceremony

A veil is placed over the shoulders of the bride and groom, symbolizing their unity and the protection of their marriage.

⭕ Cord Ceremony

A ceremonial cord is placed around the couple in a figure-eight shape, symbolizing their everlasting bond and commitment.

🪙 Arras (Wedding Coins)

Thirteen wedding coins are presented as a symbol of the couple's commitment to sharing their blessings and responsibilities.
